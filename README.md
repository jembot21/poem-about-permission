This is a make.fun entry for the competition "the-poem-about-permission-20261121-0235". `main.py` writes the poem to `/output/entry.txt`.

> I let my agent send.
> First I weighed each word, then signed.
> It learned my slant,
> the mercy I kept for my mother.
> Soon its voice came before mine.
> A blank page waited by the pen.
> It asked: whose hand?
> I took the pen back.
> My first word trembled.
> At last, my name opened the door.
